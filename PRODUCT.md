# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Solara Plural is built for plural systems and trusted singlet friends who need
a private, dependable place to organize members, front, notes, relationships,
and system context. The primary experience is the Android app, often used
quickly and one-handed; the website and PWA provide the same capabilities with
layouts adapted to larger screens.

Users may manage hundreds of alters, use the app in emotionally sensitive
moments, and depend on clear consent and privacy boundaries. The interface must
support fast scanning without turning people into records or productivity
metrics.

## Product Purpose

Solara gives plural systems a warm, personal headquarters for understanding
who is present, keeping member information, recording front history, writing
private notes, and sharing only what they explicitly choose with trusted
people.

Success means the core task on each screen is immediately legible, common
actions remain reliable on Android, personal data stays private by default,
and users can make the space feel like their own without losing accessibility
or control.

## Brand Personality

Warm, protective, and expressive.

Solara should feel like a personal system headquarters: confident enough to
organize dense information, human enough to remain safe and intimate, and
distinct enough to reflect the people using it.

## Anti-references

- Clinical health records or patient-management software.
- Corporate productivity dashboards and generic SaaS admin templates.
- Childish or infantilizing mental-health interfaces.
- Decorative glassmorphism, low-contrast pastel gradients, and Apple-like
  white bubble layouts.
- Game UI that adds spectacle, rankings, or competition without serving the
  user's actual task.
- Dense screens that depend on color alone or hide essential actions behind
  unfamiliar gestures.

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity is care.** Dense system information must remain scannable and
   understandable, especially when the user has limited attention.
2. **Identity without exposure.** Personalization should help members feel
   recognizable while privacy remains opt-in, granular, and reversible.
3. **Android first, shared capability.** Design touch-first for the APK, then
   adapt the same product behavior deliberately for web and PWA.
4. **Familiar actions, distinctive character.** Keep controls and flows
   predictable; express personality through hierarchy, color, and member
   identity rather than novel affordances.
5. **Warmth over gamification.** Visual energy may help navigation and
   recognition, but never turn member roles, fronting, or care into scores.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for web surfaces and platform-native accessibility semantics
for Android. Preserve visible keyboard focus, screen-reader labels, reduced
motion, high-contrast and reduced-texture preferences, non-color state cues,
and touch targets of at least 44 by 44 pixels. Typography and layouts must
survive long names, missing avatars, large member collections, localization,
and user-selected appearance settings.
